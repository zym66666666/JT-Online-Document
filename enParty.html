5. Calling Device Permissions

When you use our products and services, we only call the following device permissions when necessary:

  • Camera and gallery permissions (iOS and Android operating systems): When you use interactive messages (chatting with customer service), settings, editing user avatars, and screenshot feedback related services, we need to apply for and obtain this permission to use the camera or read photos in the gallery, so that you can use related services. During your operation, our application or product will open your camera or read pictures or videos stored locally. We promise to only read or store information necessary for the application. Turning off camera and gallery permissions will affect the use of interactive messages, settings, editing user avatars, and screenshot feedback services, but will not affect other services.
  • Local storage permissions (Android operating system): When you use APP upgrade and information error reporting related services, we need to apply for and obtain this permission to read and write content on the storage card, so that you can upload materials and download files. During your operation, our application or product will read your local location, mobile device ID, user account, robot ID, WiFi name and password, and log file list for local preview. We promise to only read or store information necessary for the application. Turning off local storage permissions will affect APP upgrade and information error reporting services, but will not affect other services.
  • Location permissions (iOS and Android operating systems): When you use international dialing code positioning, network diagnosis, and network configuration related services, we need to apply for and obtain this permission to obtain your location, to provide you with the international dialing code for your location or configure the network. During your operation, our application or product will obtain your location and WiFi SSID information. We promise to only read or store information necessary for the application. Turning off location permissions will default the dialing code to +86, and you will be unable to use network configuration services, but it will not affect other services.
  • Local network (iOS operating system): When you use the APP to connect to the robot, we need to apply for local network permissions for WiFi configuration, to scan the robot's hotspot during network configuration through the APP for the network configuration process. We promise to only read or store information necessary for the application. Turning off this permission will cause the network configuration service to fail, but will not affect other services.

IV. How We Use Cookies and Similar Technologies

1. Cookies

To enable the normal operation of our website and services, we sometimes place small data files called cookies on your computer, mobile device, or other devices. Cookies are small data files that a website or mobile application places on your computer, mobile device, or other device when you visit that website. It allows the website to remember your actions and preferences (such as login, shopping cart, or other preferences) over a period of time, so you don't have to re-enter them every time you return to the site or move from one page to another. We do not use cookies for any purpose other than those stated in this Policy.

If your browser or browser add-on services allow, you can modify your acceptance of cookies or refuse our cookies.

2. Cookie Similar Technologies

In addition to cookies, we may also use other similar technologies such as web beacons and pixel tags on the website. For example, emails we send to you may contain URLs that link to content on our website. If you click on that link, we track that click to help us understand your product or service preferences and improve customer service. Web beacons are usually transparent images embedded in websites or emails. With pixel tags in emails, we can tell if an email has been opened. If you do not wish your activity to be tracked in this way, you can unsubscribe from our mailing list at any time.
